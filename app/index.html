🔒 TapeLock

Milestone escrow for commissioned TapeOut circuits on X Layer. A buyer locks funds; the seller delivers a taped-out circuit; the escrow verifies the tape-out on-chain (TapeOut registry) and releases the funds. No delivery by the deadline → refund.

Demo network: X Layer Testnet (chain 1952). Built for the X Layer TapeOut Accelerate hackathon. No real funds involved.
